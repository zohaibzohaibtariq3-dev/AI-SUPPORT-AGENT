TITLE: About TechNova
TechNova is an online electronics and gadgets store. We sell laptops, headphones,
smartwatches, and accessories. Our support team is available Monday to Saturday,
9 AM to 9 PM (PKT). This document contains our official policies. The AI
assistant must only answer policy questions using the information below.

TITLE: Shipping Policy
We offer standard and express shipping across Pakistan. Standard shipping takes
3 to 5 business days and costs Rs. 250. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business
days and costs Rs. 600. Orders above Rs. 5000 qualify for free standard
shipping. Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking number by email
and SMS. We currently do not ship internationally.

TITLE: Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of delivery if the product is
unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the original invoice.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business
days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Shipping charges are
non-refundable unless the item was defective or the wrong item was sent.
Digital products and gift cards are not eligible for refunds.

TITLE: Cancellation Policy
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 2 hours of placing the order,
as long as the order has not yet been shipped. To cancel, the customer can use
the "Cancel Order" option in their account or contact support with the order
number. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but it may
be eligible for a return under the Refund Policy after delivery.

TITLE: Warranty Policy
All electronics sold by TechNova come with a manufacturer warranty of 12
months from the date of purchase, unless stated otherwise on the product page.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects but does not cover accidental
damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs. To make a warranty claim,
customers should contact support with their order number and a description of
the issue.

TITLE: Payment Methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, bank transfers, and cash on delivery
(COD) for orders within Pakistan. COD is available for orders under Rs.
50,000. Online payments are processed securely and TechNova does not store
full card details on its servers.

TITLE: Order Tracking
Customers can track their order status (Processing, Shipped, Delivered, or
Cancelled) by providing their order number to support, or by logging into
their account and visiting the "My Orders" page. Tracking numbers become
active within 24 hours of shipment.

TITLE: Exchange Policy
Exchanges are allowed within 7 days of delivery for a different size or color
of the same product, subject to availability. If the desired variant is
unavailable, the customer will be offered a refund instead. The item being
exchanged must be unused and in its original packaging.

TITLE: Contact and Escalation
If a customer's issue cannot be resolved by the AI assistant, or if the
customer explicitly asks to speak with a human agent, a support ticket should
be created and the customer should be told that a human support agent will
follow up, typically within 24 hours on business days.
